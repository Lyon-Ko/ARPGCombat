# Combat presentation direction

Use Kwang for the player and Greystone for the Boss. Native realistic armor/materials and sword animations are the final visual baseline. Keep original pack assets unchanged; adaptations belong under /Game/Combat. Manny armor is a temporary pipeline diagnostic only.

Arena remains exactly the requested plain flat platform and four walls. Use restrained neutral concrete or stone, rough material, no floor grid, ornaments, terrain, pillars or loose props. Neutral daylight with soft cool ambient fill should keep armor readable from the camera. Fixed exposure and no depth of field during gameplay. Avoid a dark scene that hides timing. Player and Boss begin facing each other at approximately 10–12 m separation with both visible after initial camera setup.

Player combat color is pale cyan/steel, Boss is muted ember/orange. Use short thin sword ribbons following the actual blade tip, brief sparks at the actual hit point, a concise metallic parry flash and a distinguishable release flash on the charged AOE. The area warning is a crisp circular boundary with low-opacity interior, not an opaque scaled cylinder. The flash starts 0.18 s before damage, and neither phase two nor hitstop may shorten this reaction interval without explicit timing validation. Sword waves have a readable lateral arc with a visible leading edge and short fading tail. Limit shake so the player can still read follow-up attacks.

Editable UMG layout at 1920×1080: player HP bottom-left above a compact control legend, Boss name/HP/poise centered near the top, phase caption below the Boss bars, lock cue bottom-right. Chinese text must use an available CJK font and stay legible without overlap. Player title 霜刃; Boss title 烬锋 · 铁卫. Riposte text should say 精准格挡 · 左键反击 (not execution, because it need not kill). Pause/result overlay gets a quiet translucent backdrop with centered text. Avoid raw class names, tags or debug logs in ordinary gameplay.

Audio: distinct swing, metal contact, successful parry, dash, wave release, heavy landing and defeat. Apply master volume to the actual playback route. Music remains below impacts. Use measured short hitstop for confirmed contact, not every warning or animation start. The final review must inspect representative strike, block, AOE, sword wave and result screenshots, and also observe motion in PIE; static renders alone cannot establish animation quality.
